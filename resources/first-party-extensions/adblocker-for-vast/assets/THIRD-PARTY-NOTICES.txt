https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock/tree/6dd2d95e50d134a477a4e183343c0b26e9147123
The included source/compile-adblock-resources.mjs reproduces resources.json
without minification. See source/README.md for reproduction instructions.

which the engine adapter does not enforce. Function bodies are unmodified.

source/upstream-resources.json from the immutable Ghostery resource revision:
https://github.com/ghostery/adblocker/tree/ff8ed92b3648bdf49ba884b7d90c46d3dda4c366/packages/adblocker/assets

Resources only change in reviewed extension package releases. Filter subscriptions cannot
replace these JavaScript bodies. The product does not download executable
resources or contact upstream analytics at runtime.
